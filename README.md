## 🎯 Objective
Analyze Amazon sales data across 200 orders and 85 customers to uncover category performance, regional trends, customer purchase behavior, and target achievement — supporting inventory, marketing, and sales strategy decisions.

## 🔑 Key Insights
- Total Sales: 26M across 200 orders and 85 unique customers
- Home Appliances led category sales at 42.06%, followed by Electronics at 37.21%
- East region generated the highest sales (6.12M) and beat its target by 2%
- North region outperformed target the most (1.04x), while West underperformed slightly (0.98x)
- Smart TV, Camera, and Air Conditioner were both the top-selling and highest-priced products (>50,000)
- Customer purchases ranged widely, with top customers contributing over 500K in total purchases

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI** — multi-page report design, DAX measures (DISTINCTCOUNT, COUNTROWS), drill-through, treemap and target-line visuals
- **Excel/Python** — data cleaning and preprocessing

## 📁 Report Pages
1. **Sales Overview** — KPIs, monthly trend, category and regional breakdown
2. **Customer Insights** — product vs region matrix, customer-wise purchases, customer distribution by region
3. **Category Analysis** — category/quantity breakdown, treemap of region × category
4. **Product Analysis & Order Trends** — premium product filtering, target vs actual performance by region

## 📈 How to Use
1. Download `Amazon_Sales_Dashboard.pbix`
2. Open in Power BI Desktop
3. Navigate between pages using the in-report buttons or bottom tabs
4. Use Region, Month, and Year slicers to filter any page
